Subsonic Player
A pro-grade player for desktop and mobile

Connect your own music server (Subsonic / Navidrome / Gonic / Jellyfin / Emby / Plex / Synology AudioStation) and enjoy a Roon-style browsing experience, a 10-band EQ, karaoke-scrolling lyrics and gapless playback. Desktop and native Android apps, free and open source.

Features

Full-featured on desktop, reimagined for portrait phones — one library, two shapes.

Browsing & discovery

Artist / album / song browsing, cover wall and global search. The Discover page offers random picks and smart recommendations based on your favorites and genre affinity, plus recently added, most played, top rated, and browsing by genre or decade.

Playback & audio

HTTP streaming, queue management and four play modes. Gapless playback with 0–15s crossfade (BASS mixer), a 10-band graphic EQ with presets (rock / pop / classical / vocal / bass boost, import & export) plus reverb, echo, chorus and compressor DSP.

Karaoke lyrics

Synchronized server lyrics first, with a parallel fallback to LRCLIB and NetEase Cloud Music. The current line is highlighted and enlarged while neighbours fade — cached locally so lyrics appear instantly next time.

Multi-protocol

Native Subsonic / OpenSubsonic (Navidrome, Gonic, Jellyfin), plus Emby, Plex and Synology AudioStation. Configure several servers with LAN-first, WAN-fallback address handling.

Library management

Create, rename and edit playlists, add songs to playlists, favorites (heart), 1–5 star ratings, share links and original-file downloads. Play history with scrobbling. The Android app also caches every track it plays for zero-data replays, and marks cached tracks as “Local” on the player page and in the play queue.

Native Android app

A fully native client with background playback, notification and lock-screen controls, headset buttons and proper audio focus. Bottom navigation and a full-screen player rebuilt for portrait phones, with resume-where-you-left-off.

Themes

Six built-in themes (Deep Black, Moonlight White, Forest Green, Midnight Blue, Sunset Orange, Rose Purple) with selectable accent colours and instant light/dark switching.

Private by design

No accounts, no cloud library — all data stays on your own server. Credentials are encrypted at rest (Windows DPAPI / Android KeyStore) and never hard-coded or uploaded.

FAQ

Is Subsonic Player free?
Yes. The client is free and open source (MIT). The BASS audio engine is an Un4seen product, free for personal non-commercial use; commercial use requires a separate license.
Which music servers are supported?
Native Subsonic / OpenSubsonic support (Navidrome, Gonic, Jellyfin and other compatible servers), plus Emby, Plex and Synology AudioStation. Multiple servers can be configured and switched with one tap.
Why is the Windows download so large?
The desktop app bundles Chromium (CEF) for its interface and ships the .NET runtime, so you do not need to install anything else — just unzip and run.
Which audio formats does the Android app support?
Bundled decoders (BASS core + add-ons): mp3, m4a (AAC), FLAC, WAV, OGG, plus DSD (.dsf), APE, WavPack and Opus — playable even when the server does not transcode. Servers often omit the file suffix for APE/DSD files; the client falls back to the MIME type.
Is my data uploaded anywhere?
No. There is no account system: every request goes straight to your own server, and server passwords are encrypted on-device (Windows DPAPI, Android KeyStore).
Does it support lyrics?
Yes. Synchronized server lyrics are preferred, with automatic fallback to LRCLIB and NetEase Cloud Music, cached locally. Both desktop and Android offer karaoke-style scrolling highlights.
